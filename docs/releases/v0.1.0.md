The first release of **Lumen Presenter**: free, offline software for showing song lyrics, scripture, media and slides in worship services. No accounts, licence keys or internet needed.

This is an early release for churches and volunteers willing to try it in rehearsals and services, and tell us what goes wrong. It has been tested by automated tests on Windows, macOS and Linux, but not yet in many real services.

Step-by-step instructions for each system are in the [README](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen#install).

**The Windows and macOS installers are not code-signed yet.** The first time, Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (click **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and macOS says it cannot check the app (on macOS 15, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**; on older versions, right-click the app and choose **Open**).

## What it does

- **Songs:** import plain text, OpenLyrics, and libraries from OpenLP, EasyWorship 6/7 and ProPresenter 5/6; sections split into slides; arrangements; search by title, author or lyrics.
- **Scripture:** type `jn 3 16-18` and press Enter. Ten free Bibles are included: English (WEB, KJV, BSB, ASV), Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Swahili, French and Spanish.
- **Media and presentations:** videos and pictures as slides or looping backgrounds; PowerPoint and PDF imported as slides.
- **Service plans:** saved as you go, daily backups, crash recovery, and `.lumen` files to carry a service to another computer.
- **Live control:** layers, fades, Clear, Logo and Black, keyboard shortcuts, timers and countdowns, messages ("Parent of 42…"), lower thirds and logos.
- **Screens:** the first time, every screen shows its number and you choose which is the main output and which the stage display; Lumen remembers it.
- **Outputs:** the audience screen runs in its own process, so a frozen operator window never freezes the screen; a stage display; a stream output with transparency.
- **On the local network (off until turned on):** a phone and tablet remote, MIDI and OSC control, and sharing plans between computers.
- **Slide editor** and **timecode cues** that follow a backing track.

## Known limits

- Not yet used on many real Windows PCs, Macs, MIDI controllers or multi-screen setups. [docs/status.md](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen/blob/main/docs/status.md) lists exactly what has been tested how.
- ProPresenter 7 documents, SongSelect import and SDI (DeckLink) output are not available yet. NDI output is experimental and off by default.
- The interface is in English only.
