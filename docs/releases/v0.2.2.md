**Lumen Presenter 0.2.2** finishes the side by side scripture layout and makes slide previews fit. It includes everything in 0.2.1 (free songs, 44 Bibles, two operators on one screen, word search, audio, auto-advance, printing). Your library, plans and settings open as they are.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.2-windows-x64.msi` |
| macOS 12 or newer (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.2-macos.dmg` |
| Linux, any distribution | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.2-x86_64.AppImage` or `Lumen-Presenter.flatpak` |
| Debian 13, Ubuntu 25.04 or newer | `lumen-presenter_0.2.2_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora | `lumen-presenter-0.2.2-1.x86_64.rpm` |

Install over the old version; your songs, plans and settings are kept. The Windows and macOS installers are not code-signed yet (Windows: **More info → Run anyway**; macOS: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**). See the [README](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen#install).

## Changed

- **Side by side scripture:** under each passage, its own reference and its own copyright line; the pair sits where a normal slide's text sits. Plan items saved earlier are laid out the new way when the plan opens.
- **Slide cards fit:** cards in the slide grid draw side by side and editor slides as they are, and long lyrics shrink to fit the card instead of being cut off.
- **Scripture preview:** the Scripture window's preview is the slide exactly as the output draws it.
- **Audio:** the main output opens the computer's sound only while music plays.

For everything new in 0.2.1, see its [release notes](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen/blob/main/docs/releases/v0.2.1.md).
