**Lumen Presenter 0.2.1** adds a free song library, 44 Bibles, two operators on one screen, and four features churches know from EasyWorship. Your library, plans and settings from 0.2.0 open as they are.

## Download

| Your computer | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.1-windows-x64.msi` |
| macOS 12 or newer (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.1-macos.dmg` |
| Linux, any distribution | `Lumen-Presenter-0.2.1-x86_64.AppImage` or `Lumen-Presenter.flatpak` |
| Debian 13, Ubuntu 25.04 or newer | `lumen-presenter_0.2.1_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora | `lumen-presenter-0.2.1-1.x86_64.rpm` |

To update, install the new version over the old one; your songs, plans and settings are kept. Step-by-step instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen#install). The installers are larger than before (about 70 MB more) because of the extra Bibles.

**The Windows and macOS installers are still not code-signed.** The first time, Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (click **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and macOS says it cannot check the app (on macOS 15, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**; on older versions, right-click the app and choose **Open**).

## New

- **About 1,200 free hymns and songs, with chords,** in every library: public domain and Creative Commons songs from WorshipCommons and the OpenLP collection, in English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Russian, Hungarian, Malayalam and Albanian. They are added a few seconds after Lumen first starts.
- **44 Bibles** (was 10): 15 English versions; Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, Twi (Akuapem and Asante), Ewe, Swahili, Lingala, Luganda, Chichewa, Shona, Kikuyu, Dholuo and Ndebele; French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Dutch, Russian, Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese, Hindi and Malayalam. Eight show as buttons; **All translations** lists every one by language, with a filter.
- **Find verses by their words:** type `love one another` (or a phrase in quotes) in the Scripture window; accents and tone marks do not matter.
- **Two operators, one screen:** a second computer (a laptop running scripture, say) can put its slides on the projector computer's screen, from the Share panel. Both see the same Live view; the phone remote keeps working.
- **Audio:** music and other audio files play beside the slides, for background music before the service, with Play once or Loop, and Stop in the Layers list.
- **Auto-advance:** announcement slides move on by themselves every 5 to 30 seconds, round and round.
- **Printing:** the order of service, lyrics or chord charts as a PDF to print (Plan ▾ → Print…).
- **Your own logo:** any picture or video as the Logo (F6).
- **Bring your own Bibles:** besides USFM and USFX, Lumen now reads Zefania XML, OpenSong XML and OpenLP CSV, so Bibles exported from OpenLP, OpenSong or EasyWorship come across (only show Bibles you have the right to use).
- **Interface in French, Spanish and Portuguese** (Plan ▾ → Language).

## Changed and fixed

- Side by side scripture is placed like a normal slide, with each translation's reference under its own passage. The copyright line can be hidden for public-domain Bibles; Creative Commons Bibles keep a short credit on the first slide, as their licence asks.
- Timers show on the stage display only, never on the audience screen (countdown slides still show to the audience).
- Clicking outside any menu or panel closes it, and that click does nothing else; the toolbar (Clear, Logo, Black) always acts at once.
- Videos and pictures can be removed from the library (the file stays on your disk).
- Hindi and Malayalam Bibles use bundled fonts, so their text fits the screen on every computer.

## Known limits

- Still not used in many real services or on many Windows PCs and Macs. [docs/status.md](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen/blob/main/docs/status.md) lists what has been tested how.
- Two operators, one screen has been tested between two copies of Lumen on one computer, not yet across a church network.
- Placing outputs on the right screen under Wayland has not been tried with a real projector yet.
- Chinese and Korean Bibles use the computer's own fonts; Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu Bibles are not included yet.
- Copyrighted Bibles and songs (NIV, NKJV, modern worship songs) are not included; a church with a licence can import its own.
