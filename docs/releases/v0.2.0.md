The second release of **Lumen Presenter**: free, offline software for showing song lyrics, scripture, media and slides in worship services. No accounts, licence keys or internet needed.

This release follows a full check of 0.1.0. It fixes what that check found, makes the everyday workflow quicker, and adds the last features planned for the first version: video controls, song usage for CCLI reports, and chords. Your library and plans from 0.1.0 open as they are.

To update, install the new version over the old one; your songs, plans and settings are kept. Step-by-step instructions are in the [README](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen#install).

**The Windows and macOS installers are still not code-signed.** The first time, Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (click **More info**, then **Run anyway**), and macOS says it cannot check the app (on macOS 15, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**; on older versions, right-click the app and choose **Open**).

## New

- **Preview first, then live.** A click on a song, video, picture, scripture or plan item puts it in Preview; a double-click puts it on the screen at once. Slides in the grid still go live with one click.
- **Drag and drop.** Drag songs, videos, pictures and presentations from the library onto the service plan, where you want them, and drag plan items up or down to reorder. **+ Add** beside the plan shows where to find each kind of item.
- **Scripture in its own window,** so you can keep working while it is open. The reference stays in three parts (book, chapter, verse): type `j` and John fills in, Space moves on, ← goes back. New: a **second translation side by side** (English and Yoruba, say), **Fit to slide**, and the reference **below, above, or with the text in the lower third** for streams.
- **Video controls.** While a video is on the screen, **Pause**, **Play** and a bar to jump along it. **Start** and **End** points trim a video, and **Play once** or **Loop** is kept with each video.
- **Slide transitions:** Cut, Fade, Push, Push up, Wipe and Zoom.
- **Chords.** Type chords in the lyrics (`[G]Amazing [C]grace`) or keep them from imported songs. The audience never sees them; the stage display's new **Chords** layout shows them to the band, and **-** / **+** transpose a song for the band.
- **Song usage for CCLI reports.** Every song shown is counted once a day, on this computer only. **Plan ▾ → Song usage (CCLI)…** saves a spreadsheet to submit to CCLI yourself.
- **Song editor:** **Auto-split slides** and **Edit arrangement** (a chorus after every verse, a verse left out).
- **Screens:** each output checks which screen it is really on, and Live says when a video cannot be played or an output is not where it should be. On Linux under Wayland, the Screens panel can place outputs through XWayland.
- **T** jumps to the Tag of a song, alongside C, B, P and E.

## Fixed

- The safety keys (F1 to F7: Clear, Logo, Black) now always work, also while typing in the search box, a panel or the scripture window.
- Changes are never lost without asking: the slide editor and theme editor ask Save, Discard or Keep editing.
- Restoring a backup and turning the output off now ask first.
- Starting Lumen a second time brings the open window forward, instead of two copies sharing one output.
- Saving a song's lyrics no longer drops a repeated chorus from its order.
- Previous (←) works after moving on to the next item; Page Down and Page Up skip headers.
- Escape closes the plan menus; actions that cannot work are greyed out; failures say what went wrong.
- Removing a timer, prop or cue asks **Sure?** first.
- Props' corner pictures (renamed from "logos", so they are not confused with the full-screen Logo) are copied into Lumen's own folder, so moving the original does not break them.
- Recent messages follow the Ticker choice; Share, Remote and Updates open one at a time; Enter moves through the Props and Timers forms; layouts fit small windows.

## Known limits

- Still not used in many real services, or on many Windows PCs, Macs, MIDI controllers and multi-screen setups. [docs/status.md](https://github.com/OrisabinoneDanielO/Lumen/blob/main/docs/status.md) lists what has been tested how.
- Placing outputs on the right screen under Wayland (GNOME, KDE) has not been tried with a real projector yet.
- ProPresenter 7 documents, SongSelect import and SDI (DeckLink) output are not available yet. NDI output is experimental and off by default.
- The interface is in English only.
