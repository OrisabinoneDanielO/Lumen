# Code of conduct

Lumen Presenter is made by and for people who serve in churches and anyone else who wants to help. We want working on it to be welcoming for everyone, whatever their background, experience, denomination or none.

**Be kind and patient.** Many contributors are volunteers, and some are new to open source. Explain rather than dismiss; assume good intent.

**Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention or language, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats).

**Keep it about the work.** Debates about theology or politics belong elsewhere; here we make software that serves every congregation.

**Respect privacy.** Do not share others' personal information, or song libraries and recordings you do not have the right to share.

If something here makes you uncomfortable, or you see behaviour that breaks these guidelines, contact the maintainers privately (through GitHub, to the repository owner). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who do not follow this code.
